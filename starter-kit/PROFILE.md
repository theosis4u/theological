# Profile

Give this file to your AI assistant along with `METHOD.md`. Answer the prompts in plain sentences; delete the prompts when done. Keep this repo private.

## Tradition and background
- What tradition or church do you belong to, if any? How long?
- Which other traditions do you engage most often, and in what setting (study group, debate, online)?

## Positions you have reached
- On which questions have you landed, and on which are you still undecided?
- Which questions are you studying right now?

## Sources you trust
- Default Bible translation and why. Comparison translations.
- Study Bibles, commentaries, catechisms, or confessions you rely on.
- Scholars you read most. Voices you enjoy but want checked.

## How you want answers
- Audience (yourself, a class, a friend, social media).
- Tone and length preferences. Things you dislike in AI answers.

## Tools
- Do you use Logos (desktop, web, both)? Other software?
- Where do you share study material with others?

## Open questions for the assistant
- List anything you want clarified before work begins.
