# Other Faiths and Secular Worldviews

This repo grew out of Christian Bible study, but the method works for anyone: members of other faiths, people exploring, and atheists, agnostics, and skeptics. This page gives brief starting points and shows how to build a full pack of your own. Each sketch is deliberately short; describe your own view as its thoughtful adherents would, and correct anything here that doesn't match what you hold.

## How to build your own pack

Copy any Christian pack in this folder and keep the same sections, renamed where needed:

1. **Authoritative sources:** the texts, traditions, or methods your view treats as authoritative (scriptures, legal or commentary traditions, creeds, philosophical works, scientific or historical method).
2. **Key thinkers:** historic and modern voices your view respects most.
3. **Study resources:** editions, commentaries, reference works, and free sites.
4. **Text preferences:** which translations or editions you read, and in what language.
5. **Slot text: "your view first":** one paragraph for section 4 of the [method template](../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md).

Then use the Christian packs (and any others) as **comparison and counter-argument sources**, quoted from their own primary texts.

## Starting sketches

### Judaism
- **Sources:** the Tanakh (Hebrew Bible), the Mishnah and Talmud, midrash, and the classical commentators (e.g. Rashi, Ibn Ezra, Ramban), with codes such as Maimonides' *Mishneh Torah*. Movements (Orthodox, Conservative, Reform, and others) weigh these differently.
- **Free:** [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/) (texts and translations with linked commentaries).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Jewish reading, citing the Tanakh in Hebrew where it matters and rabbinic sources by tractate and folio or by book and section. Say which movement or commentator a reading comes from. Note where Christian readings of the same passage differ, from Christian sources.*

### Islam
- **Sources:** the Qur'an, the hadith collections (e.g. Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim), tafsir (commentary), and the schools of law and theology. Sunni and Shi'a traditions differ in hadith sources and authorities.
- **Free:** [Quran.com](https://quran.com/) (Arabic text with several translations), [Sunnah.com](https://sunnah.com/) (hadith collections).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Islamic reading, citing the Qur'an by surah and verse and hadith by collection and number. Say whether a view is Sunni or Shi'a and which school it reflects. Where the Bible is discussed, quote it from Christian or Jewish sources.*

### Other religions
Buddhist, Hindu, Sikh, Baha'i, and other traditions can be set up the same way. Free text collections include [Access to Insight](https://www.accesstoinsight.org/) (Theravada Buddhist texts) and [sacred-texts.com](https://sacred-texts.com/) (older public-domain translations; check the edition before relying on it).

### Secular: atheist, agnostic, skeptic, or humanist
- **Sources:** philosophy of religion, historical-critical biblical scholarship, the history and archaeology of the ancient Near East and the Greco-Roman world, and the scientific method. Name the scholars you rely on rather than "scholars say."
- **Free:** the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/) and the [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/) (peer-reviewed entries on arguments for and against theism, religious epistemology, and more); the free Bible sites in [Free resources](../guides/free-resources.md) for the texts themselves.
- **Slot text:** *Lead with a secular, evidence-based reading. Distinguish what the text says, what historians can establish about its origins, and what religious traditions claim about it. When engaging a religious tradition, state its view as its best scholars would and quote its own sources. Apply the same fallacy review to skeptical arguments as to religious ones.*

## Ground rules

- Describe every view, including ones you reject, in terms its own adherents would recognize.
- Quote primary texts with exact references; never put words in a tradition's mouth.
- Run the fallacy review on every argument, your own side's included: steelman, don't strawman.
