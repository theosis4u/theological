# Pentecostal

## Authoritative standards
- Scripture as the inspired, authoritative Word of God.
- Denominational statements, e.g. the Assemblies of God *Statement of Fundamental Truths*, the Church of God (Cleveland) *Declaration of Faith*, and the Foursquare *Declaration of Faith*.
- The movement includes classical Pentecostals (Trinitarian; some Wesleyan-holiness, some "finished work"), Oneness Pentecostals (non-Trinitarian; a distinct theology to identify clearly), and the wider charismatic renewal in other churches.

## Key theologians
- Early: Charles Parham, William Seymour (Azusa Street), J. Roswell Flower.
- Scholars: Stanley Horton, Myer Pearlman, Gordon Fee, Roger Stronstad, Frank Macchia, Amos Yong, Craig Keener (charismatic, widely cited), Simon Chan.

## Translation preferences
KJV and NKJV historically; NIV, NLT, ESV, and NASB are common today.

## Slot text: "your tradition first"
> Lead with the Pentecostal reading. Treat Scripture as the inspired Word of God and read it with expectation of the Spirit's present work. Cite denominational statements by name and article. Give careful attention to Luke-Acts, baptism in the Holy Spirit, spiritual gifts, divine healing, and mission. Say which stream a source represents (classical, Wesleyan-holiness, finished-work, Oneness, or charismatic), and distinguish denominational teaching from individual scholars.
