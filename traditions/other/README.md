## Overview

Brief entries for traditions not covered by a full pack. Each can be expanded into its own pack using the same sections (see the [traditions and worldviews index](../README.md)).

| Tradition | Core in its own terms | Main branches | Primary texts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Taoism (Daoism) | Harmony with the Dao, the way of nature; wu wei (effortless action) | Philosophical; religious (Zhengyi, Quanzhen) | *Daodejing*, *Zhuangzi*, the Daozang |
| Confucianism | Moral cultivation, ritual propriety, and right relationships | Classical; Neo-Confucian (Zhu Xi, Wang Yangming) | *Analects*, *Mencius*, the Five Classics |
| Shinto | Reverence for the kami (sacred powers) through shrine ritual and purity | Shrine Shinto; folk Shinto; Shinto sects | *Kojiki*, *Nihon Shoki* (not scripture in the same sense) |
| Baha'i Faith | One God, the unity of religions and humanity, progressive revelation | One community (the Universal House of Justice) | Writings of Baha'u'llah, the Bab, and 'Abdu'l-Baha |
| Zoroastrianism | Ahura Mazda, the struggle of good and evil, good thoughts, words, and deeds | Iranian Zoroastrians; Parsis of India | The Avesta, especially the Gathas |

## Taoism and Confucianism

- **Free sources:** [Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/) (classical Chinese texts with translations), [sacred-texts.com: Taoism](https://sacred-texts.com/tao/index.htm), [sacred-texts.com: Confucianism](https://sacred-texts.com/cfu/index.htm).
- **Slot text (Taoist):** *Lead with the Taoist reading, citing the Daodejing by chapter and the Zhuangzi by chapter, and say whether you mean philosophical or religious Taoism.*
- **Slot text (Confucian):** *Lead with the Confucian reading, citing the Analects by book and passage and naming the Neo-Confucian school if relevant.*

## Shinto

- **Free sources:** [Jinja Honcho (Association of Shinto Shrines)](https://www.jinjahoncho.or.jp/en/), [sacred-texts.com: Shinto](https://sacred-texts.com/shi/index.htm).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Shinto reading, centering practice and the kami rather than doctrine, and citing the Kojiki or Nihon Shoki where myths are discussed.*

## Baha'i Faith

- **Free sources:** [Baha'i Reference Library](https://www.bahai.org/library/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Baha'i reading, citing the Writings of Baha'u'llah and authoritative interpretations, and noting how it views earlier religions as stages of one revelation.*

## Zoroastrianism

- **Free sources:** [sacred-texts.com: Zoroastrianism](https://sacred-texts.com/zor/index.htm) (older translations of the Avesta). Avesta.org did not load when checked.
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Zoroastrian reading, citing the Gathas by Yasna and stanza, and noting differences between Iranian and Parsi communities where relevant.*
