# Lutheran

## Authoritative standards
- Scripture as the norm that norms (*norma normans*); the confessions as the norm that is normed (*norma normata*).
- The *Book of Concord* (1580): the three ecumenical creeds, Augsburg Confession and its Apology, Smalcald Articles, Treatise on the Power and Primacy of the Pope, Small and Large Catechisms, Formula of Concord. Cite as AC IV, Ap IV, SD II, etc.

## Key theologians
- Reformation and orthodoxy: Martin Luther, Philip Melanchthon, Martin Chemnitz, Johann Gerhard.
- Modern: C. F. W. Walther, Francis Pieper, Hermann Sasse, Dietrich Bonhoeffer, Robert Kolb, Oswald Bayer.

## Slot text: "your tradition first"
> Lead with the Lutheran reading. Treat Scripture as the norm that norms and the Book of Concord as its faithful exposition, cited by article. Read through the distinction of Law and Gospel and justification by grace through faith for Christ's sake, and give attention to the means of grace: Word and Sacraments. Note where Lutheran church bodies differ, and distinguish confessional teaching from individual theologians.
