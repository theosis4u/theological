# Wesleyan / Methodist

## Authoritative standards
- Scripture as primary, with tradition, reason, and experience (often called the "Wesleyan quadrilateral").
- The Articles of Religion (Wesley's abridgment of the Thirty-Nine Articles) and, in some bodies, the Confession of Faith of the Evangelical United Brethren.
- Wesley's *Standard Sermons* and *Explanatory Notes upon the New Testament*.
- Denominational books of discipline (e.g. the United Methodist *Book of Discipline*, Global Methodist and Wesleyan Church disciplines; Nazarene *Manual*).

## Key theologians
- John Wesley, Charles Wesley (hymns), John Fletcher, Adam Clarke, Richard Watson, William Burt Pope, H. Orton Wiley, Thomas Oden, Albert Outler, Randy Maddox, Kenneth Collins.

## Slot text: "your tradition first"
> Lead with the Wesleyan reading. Treat Scripture as primary, interpreted with tradition, reason, and experience. Draw on Wesley's sermons and notes and the Articles of Religion. Frame salvation through prevenient, justifying, and sanctifying grace, with attention to assurance and the call to holiness of heart and life (Christian perfection). Note where Methodist and holiness bodies differ.
