## About these packs

The method in this repo works for any faith or none. These packs cover the main families of belief beyond the [Christian packs](../README.md). Branches within a family are **not** interchangeable: they differ in texts, authorities, and beliefs. So each family gives every major branch its own subsection with its distinctive beliefs, key texts and authorities, free sources, and its own **slot text**, so you can pick a specific branch as your primaries. Each family also has a comparison table and notes where a branch's status is disputed by others.

> **Review note:** these packs were drafted as respectful outsider summaries. Adherents should review and correct them. Free sources were checked on 2026-10-12; a few sites block automated checks, and those are noted.

## How to use a pack

1. Pick your **branch**, not just the family, and copy its slot text into section 4 of your [method template](../../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md). Edit it to match what you actually hold.
2. Set your branch's texts and authorities as your **primaries**, or ask your AI assistant to find them.
3. Use sibling branches, other families, and the Christian packs as **comparison and counter-argument** sources, quoted from their own texts.
4. Keep the fallacy review on every argument, your own side's included.

## How to build your own pack

Copy any pack and keep the same sections: overview in adherents' own terms; branches at a glance (a comparison table); one subsection per branch with distinctive beliefs, key texts and authorities, free sources, and slot text; primary vs. comparison sources; points of dialogue. See [CONTRIBUTING](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Ground rules

- Describe every view, including ones you reject, in terms its own adherents would recognize.
- Note disputed status neutrally: say who disputes it and why, without taking a side.
- Quote primary texts with exact references; never put words in a tradition's mouth.
- Steelman, don't strawman.
