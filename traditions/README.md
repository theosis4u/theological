## About these packs

The method in this repo works for any faith or none. Each family below is a peer folder with its own pack; none is the default and none is the "house view" of this repo. Branches within a family are **not** interchangeable: they differ in texts, authorities, and beliefs. So each pack gives every major branch (or tradition) its own section and its own **slot text** for section 4 of the [method template](../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md).

> **Review note:** packs are drafted as respectful summaries that describe each view as its own adherents would. Adherents should review and correct them. Free sources were checked on 2026-10-12; a few sites block automated checks, and those are noted.

## How to use a pack

1. Pick your **branch**, not just the family, and copy its slot text into section 4 of your [method template](../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md). Edit it to match what you actually hold.
2. Set your branch's texts and authorities as your **primaries**, or ask your AI assistant to find them.
3. Use sibling branches and other families as **comparison and counter-argument** sources, quoted from their own texts.
4. Keep the fallacy review on every argument, your own side's included.

## How to build your own pack

Copy any pack and keep the same sections: overview in adherents' own terms; branches at a glance; one section per branch with distinctive beliefs, key texts and authorities, free sources, and slot text; primary vs. comparison sources; points of dialogue. Put it in its own folder under `traditions/`, or in an existing family folder, and add it to the table above. See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Ground rules

- Describe every view, including ones you reject, in terms its own adherents would recognize.
- Note disputed status neutrally: say who disputes it and why, without taking a side.
- Quote primary texts with exact references; never put words in a tradition's mouth.
- Steelman, don't strawman.
