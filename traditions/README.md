# Tradition Starter Packs

Each pack helps you adapt the [method template](../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md) to a tradition: its key standards, voices, study Bibles, translation habits, and a ready paragraph for the template's "your tradition first" slot (section 4).

These packs describe each tradition as its own members would, without polemic. None of them is the "house view" of this repo. Pick yours, edit it to match what you actually hold, and use the others to read other traditions fairly from their own sources.

**About Logos resources:** titles are listed only where they are widely known to be available. Items marked _(verify in Logos)_ may exist only in some editions, packages, or under a different title. Availability and package contents change, so check your own library or the Logos store.

## World religions and worldviews

For other faiths and for secular worldviews, see [World religions and worldviews](world-religions/README.md): Buddhism, Hinduism, Islam, Judaism, Sikhism, Jainism, Pagan and Neopagan, secular (atheist, agnostic, humanist, naturalist, skeptic), esoteric and occult, and other traditions (Taoist, Confucian, Shinto, Baha'i, Zoroastrian, Latter-day Saint). Each family gives every major branch its own subsection and slot text, because branches differ.

To add a tradition, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
