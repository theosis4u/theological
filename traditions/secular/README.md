## Overview

Secular worldviews do not ground their outlook in religious revelation. The labels overlap: one person may be an atheist about gods, an agnostic about what can be known, a naturalist about what exists, a humanist about ethics, and a skeptic about method. There are no scriptures; authority rests on argument and evidence.

## Branches at a glance

| | Atheist | Agnostic | Humanist | Naturalist | Skeptic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core claim | No belief in gods (or a claim that none exist) | God's existence is unknown or unknowable | Ethics and meaning grounded in human welfare and reason | Only natural entities and causes exist | Proportion belief to evidence |
| Question it answers | What do you believe about gods? | What can be known? | How should we live? | What exists? | How should we decide what to believe? |

## Atheist

- **Distinctive beliefs:** lack of belief in any god ("weak" atheism) or the claim that no god exists ("strong" atheism).
- **Key thinkers:** David Hume, Bertrand Russell, J. L. Mackie (*The Miracle of Theism*), Graham Oppy.
- **Free sources:** [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: "Atheism and Agnosticism"](https://plato.stanford.edu/), [The Secular Web (Internet Infidels)](https://infidels.org/), [American Atheists](https://www.atheists.org/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with an atheist reading. Distinguish lack of belief from denial, state theistic arguments at their strongest (from their own defenders), and apply the same fallacy review to atheist arguments.*

## Agnostic

- **Distinctive beliefs:** the existence of God is unknown, or unknowable in principle (T. H. Huxley coined the term).
- **Free sources:** [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://iep.utm.edu/) (entries on religious epistemology), SEP as above.
- **Slot text:** *Lead with an agnostic reading, separating what the evidence supports from what remains open, and noting where both theists and atheists overclaim.*

## Humanist

- **Distinctive beliefs:** a non-religious life stance: reason, compassion, and human flourishing as the basis of ethics and meaning, often stated in humanist declarations.
- **Key texts and authorities:** the Amsterdam Declaration (Humanists International), the Humanist Manifestos (American Humanist Association).
- **Free sources:** [Humanists International](https://humanists.international/), [American Humanist Association](https://americanhumanist.org/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with a humanist reading, grounding ethical claims in human welfare and reason, and engaging religious ethics from its own sources.*

## Naturalist

- **Distinctive beliefs:** metaphysical naturalism: nature is all there is; methodological naturalism (in science) brackets the supernatural without necessarily denying it. Some religious people accept methodological but not metaphysical naturalism.
- **Free sources:** [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: "Naturalism"](https://plato.stanford.edu/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with a naturalist reading, stating whether the claim is metaphysical or methodological naturalism.*

## Skeptic

- **Distinctive beliefs:** scientific skepticism: beliefs should be proportioned to evidence; extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence. Skepticism is a method that can be applied by religious and non-religious people alike.
- **Free sources:** [Skeptical Inquirer](https://skepticalinquirer.org/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with a skeptical, evidence-first reading. Separate what the text says, what historians and scientists can establish, and what traditions claim, and apply the same standard to every side.*

## Primary vs comparison sources

- **Primary:** peer-reviewed philosophy (SEP, IEP), historical-critical scholarship, and science, citing named scholars rather than "scholars say."
- **Comparison:** the other families in the [traditions index](../README.md), quoted from their own primary texts and best defenders.

## Points of dialogue

- Arguments for and against God's existence (cosmological, design, moral, evil, hiddenness).
- The historical study of scripture: what historians can and can't establish.
- Grounding morality and meaning; miracles and evidence.
