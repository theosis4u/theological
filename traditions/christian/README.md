## About these packs

Each pack helps you adapt the [method template](../../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md) to one Christian tradition: its key standards, voices, study Bibles, translation habits, and a ready paragraph for the template's "your tradition first" slot (section 4). Each pack describes its tradition as its own members would, without polemic. Pick yours, edit it to match what you actually hold, and use the others, and the other families in the [traditions index](../README.md), to read other views fairly from their own sources.

**About Logos resources:** titles are listed only where they are widely known to be available. Items marked _(verify in Logos)_ may exist only in some editions, packages, or under a different title. Availability and package contents change, so check your own library or the Logos store.

### Nontrinitarian and restorationist offshoots (status disputed)

Latter-day Saints, Jehovah's Witnesses, Oneness Pentecostals, Unitarians (historic and Unitarian Universalist), and Christian Science: [nontrinitarian-offshoots.md](nontrinitarian-offshoots.md). Each regards itself as Christian (or, for many Unitarian Universalists, as a broader faith); most Catholic, Orthodox, and Protestant churches dispute this on the doctrine of the Trinity or added scripture. The page explains both sides without settling it.

### Movements across traditions

**"Evangelical" or "nondenominational"?** Evangelicalism is a movement across many traditions, not one tradition. See [Evangelical: a movement, not a tradition](evangelical.md) to find the pack that fits.

## Comparing the packs

| Tradition | Scripture and tradition | Baptism | Government |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eastern Orthodox | Scripture within Holy Tradition | Infants and adults (immersion, with chrismation) | Bishops |
| Roman Catholic | Scripture and Tradition, with the Magisterium | Infants and adults | Bishops, with the Pope |
| Moravian | Scripture the sole standard; creeds as aids | Infants and adults | Bishops and synods |
| Anabaptist | Scripture read in community | Believers | Congregational / conference |
| Anglican | Scripture, with creeds, councils, and reason | Infants and adults | Bishops |
| Lutheran | Scripture alone, with the Book of Concord | Infants and adults | Varies (bishops or synods) |
| Reformed / Presbyterian | Scripture alone, with the confessions | Infants and adults | Elders and courts |
| Baptist | Scripture alone; confessions as summaries | Believers (immersion) | Congregational |
| Quaker | The Spirit's leading, with Scripture | None (inward baptism) | Meetings |
| Wesleyan / Methodist | Scripture first, with tradition, reason, experience | Infants and adults | Bishops and conferences |
| Adventist | Scripture alone; Ellen White as a prophetic gift | Believers (immersion) | Conferences |
| Nondenominational | The church's own statement of faith | Usually believers | Usually congregational |
| Pentecostal / Charismatic | Scripture, with the Spirit's present work | Usually believers | Varies |
| Restorationist | The New Testament as pattern; no creeds | Believers (immersion) | Congregational |

Summaries are simplified; quote each pack's own standards for detail. Quietism is a movement of prayer, not a church, and the offshoots are compared on their own page.
