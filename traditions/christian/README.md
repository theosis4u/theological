## About these packs

Each pack helps you adapt the [method template](../../guides/ai-theology-method-template.md) to one Christian tradition: its key standards, voices, study Bibles, translation habits, and a ready paragraph for the template's "your tradition first" slot (section 4). Each pack describes its tradition as its own members would, without polemic. Pick yours, edit it to match what you actually hold, and use the others, and the other families in the [traditions index](../README.md), to read other views fairly from their own sources.

**About Logos resources:** titles are listed only where they are widely known to be available. Items marked _(verify in Logos)_ may exist only in some editions, packages, or under a different title. Availability and package contents change, so check your own library or the Logos store.
