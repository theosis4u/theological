## Overview

Jains follow the teachings of the 24 Tirthankaras ("ford-makers"), the last being Mahavira (6th–5th century BCE). Core teachings: every living being has a soul (jiva); karma is a subtle matter that binds the soul; liberation (moksha) comes through right faith, right knowledge, and right conduct. Ethics center on non-violence (ahimsa), non-possessiveness, and many-sidedness (anekantavada). There is no creator God; liberated souls (siddhas) are revered.

## Branches at a glance

| | Digambara | Svetambara |
|---|---|---|
| Monks | Monks renounce all clothing | Monks and nuns wear white robes |
| Women and liberation | Traditionally, women must be reborn as men to attain liberation | Women can attain liberation; the 19th Tirthankara, Mallinatha, is regarded as a woman |
| Scripture | Holds that the original canon (Agamas) was lost; uses later texts such as the *Shatkhandagama* | Accepts a surviving canon of Agamas |
| Sub-groups | Bisapanthi, Terapanthi (Digambara) | Murtipujaka (image-worshipping), Sthanakvasi and Terapanthi (non-image) |

## Digambara

- **Distinctive beliefs:** complete renunciation (including clothing) for monks; the canon was lost, so later texts by acharyas are authoritative.
- **Key texts and authorities:** *Shatkhandagama*, Kundakunda's works (e.g. *Samayasara*), Umasvati's *Tattvartha Sutra* (accepted by both branches).
- **Free sources:** [Jain eLibrary](https://www.jainelibrary.org/) (texts and scholarship from many groups), [sacred-texts.com: Jainism](https://sacred-texts.com/jai/index.htm) (older translations).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Digambara reading, citing Kundakunda and the Tattvartha Sutra, and note where Svetambara teaching differs.*

## Svetambara

- **Distinctive beliefs:** monks and nuns wear white; women can attain liberation; accepts the Agama canon. Sthanakvasi and Terapanthi sub-groups reject image worship.
- **Key texts and authorities:** the Agamas (e.g. *Acaranga Sutra*, *Kalpa Sutra*), the Tattvartha Sutra; acharyas of each sub-group.
- **Free sources:** [Jain eLibrary](https://www.jainelibrary.org/).
- **Slot text:** *Lead with the Svetambara reading of the named sub-group (Murtipujaka, Sthanakvasi, or Terapanthi), citing the Agamas, and note where Digambara teaching differs.*

## Primary vs comparison sources

- **Primary:** your branch's canon and acharyas.
- **Comparison:** the other branch, Buddhist and Hindu views of karma and liberation, and academic Jain studies.

## Points of dialogue

- Non-violence in ethics, diet, and daily life; many-sidedness as an approach to religious disagreement.
- Liberation without a creator God; karma as a substance vs. other views of karma.
