## About this file

A color-coded reading of the whole letter that tracks who is being addressed or described in each phrase: the Jewish believers who “first hoped in the Christ” (Ephesians 1:12), the Gentile “you also” (Ephesians 1:13), and the “one new man” made of both (Ephesians 2:15). The colors record one reading of the pronouns; where commentators differ, the phrase carries a gray asterisk.

- **Translation and licence.** The verse text is the *Literal Standard Version* (LSV), © 2020 Covenant Press and the Covenant Christian Coalition, from [ebible.org/englsv](https://ebible.org/englsv/) (checked verse by verse on 2026-10-18). eBible.org distributes the LSV under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://ebible.org/englsv/copyright.htm). The [Covenant Press LSV page](https://www.covenantpress.org/p/literal-standard-version.html) confirms that release but also “requests that the text remain unaltered in the English language.” Because a requested restriction sits beside a licence that permits adaptation, this file takes the stricter course: the English text is unaltered, and everything added is ours and marked. Covenant Press does not endorse the annotations. Because the file reproduces CC BY-SA text, it (text, colors and annotations) is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **What is the LSV’s and what is ours.** Square brackets holding a plain English word (for example “[is]”, “[places]”) are the LSV’s own supplied words, and “[[ ]]” at Ephesians 5:30 is the LSV’s own mark. Brackets holding *italic* words (for example “[*maybe*, holy ones\*]”, “[*see* 1:15]”), the gray italic notes, the bold-black emphasis and all colors are our study annotations.
- **Viewing.** On the website the colors use CSS classes; every colored phrase is also bold and has a hover title naming its color, so the reading survives where colors are stripped.

## Ephesians 5

**1** Become, <span class="aud-hinge" title="Red: transitional hinge"><strong>then</strong></span>, followers of God, as beloved children, **2** and walk in love, as the Christ also loved <span class="aud-both" title="Green: both / one new man"><strong>us</strong></span>, and gave Himself for <span class="aud-both" title="Green: both / one new man"><strong>us</strong></span>, an offering and a sacrifice to God for an odor of a refreshing fragrance, **3** and whoredom, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, do not let it even be named among <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span>, as is proper to <span class="aud-jew" title="Yellow: Jewish believers / Israel-tied"><strong>holy ones</strong></span> [<span class="aud-ambig" title="Gray: ambiguous"><strong>maybe, holy ones\*</strong></span>]; **4** also filthiness, and foolish talking, or jesting—the things not fit—but rather thanksgiving; **5** for <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span> know this, that every whoremonger, or unclean, or covetous person, who is an idolater, has no inheritance in the kingdom of the Christ and God. **6** Let no one deceive <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span> with vain words, for because of these things comes the anger of God on the sons of the disobedience; **7** do not become, then, partakers with them, **8** for <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span> were once darkness, and now light in the LORD; walk as children of light, **9** for the fruit of the light [is] in all goodness, and righteousness, and truth, **10** proving what is well-pleasing to the LORD; **11** and have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of the darkness and rather even convict, **12** for it is a shame even to speak of the things done by them in secret, **13** and all the things reproved by the light are revealed, for everything that is revealed is light; **14** for this reason it says, “Arouse yourself, you who are sleeping, and arise out of the dead, and the Christ will shine on you.” **15** See, then, how exactly <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span> walk, not as unwise, but as wise, **16** redeeming the time, because the days are evil; **28** so ought the husbands to love their own wives as their own bodies: he who is loving his own wife—he loves himself; **29** for no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and nurtures it, as also the LORD—the Assembly, **30** because <span class="aud-both" title="Green: both / one new man"><strong>we</strong></span> are members of His body, [[of His flesh, and of His bones.]] **31** “For this cause will a man leave his father and mother, and will be joined to his wife, and the two will be into one flesh”; **32** this secret is great, and <span class="aud-paul" title="Purple: Paul"><strong>I</strong></span> speak in regard to Christ and to the Assembly; **33** but <span class="aud-gentile" title="Blue: Gentiles / you / the nations"><strong>you</strong></span> also, everyone in particular—let each so love his own wife as himself, and the wife—that she may revere the husband.
