# Quick question

## Prompt

```text
Request type: quick question (150–400 words).
Question: <your question>
Follow METHOD.md. Answer directly in the first sentence, then give 2–5 supporting points with the key references. Lead with <your tradition>'s answer if traditions differ, and say briefly where others differ. Mark anything you could not verify.
```

## Example

```text
Request type: quick question (150–400 words).
Question: In Psalm 23:1, what does calling the LORD a "shepherd" mean in its ancient setting?
Follow METHOD.md. Answer directly in the first sentence, then give 2–5 supporting points with the key references. Lead with my tradition's answer if traditions differ, and say briefly where others differ. Mark anything you could not verify.
```

What a good answer looks like: one direct sentence (shepherd as a common royal image for a provider and protector), then points tying it to the rest of the psalm (Ps 23:2–4), to other shepherd texts (e.g. Ezek 34; John 10:11), and a note on any translation difference in verse 1.
